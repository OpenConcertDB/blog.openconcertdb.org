## Big Things
- New experiemental journal entry. You can disable it if you don’t want to use it but it’s kinda neat.
- Fixed several issues in the Concert, Festival, and Tour views
- New poster widget (Check out the posters on the Smashing Pumpkins and Weezer tours!)

### Journal
- New experimental journal entry, using Apple FoundationKit (Apple Intelligence)
  Requires an iPhone 15 Pro or higher, or an iPad with an M1 chip or newer, and Apple Intelligence to be enabled
  _**Important Reminder:** Apple Intelligence is LOCAL-ONLY models. Your text is not shared or sent anywhere, never leaves the device, and it does not make requests to the Internet. You can enable Airplane Mode and it’ll still work. Because of this, your mileage may vary, based on what device you’re using._

### Artist
- Display all 5 genres for the artist

### Concert
- You can now report duplicate concerts.

### Tour
- Improved the tour layout to make posters far less confusing
- Tour poster view now also uses Metal to render actual 3D light, providing a much more natural fade and transition.
- Tour poster view also shows you if a date is coming to you, lets you tap through to the artist pages, as well as press-and-hold to open the Apple Music preview sheet.

### Festival
- New iPad view and revamped iPhone view
- Festival poster view now also uses Metal to render actual 3D light

### Misc Bug Fixes
- Fixed the artist line up issue in the concert and festival views so they work more smoothly instead of popping around, causing visual chaos.
- Ticket view now offers guidance when empty
- “Everywhere” and “Everyone” lists now sort by attendance count, then alpha
- iOS 26 and iOS 18 no longer show banner headers where banner headers aren’t supposed to be.
