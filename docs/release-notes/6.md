## Big Things
- Search now lets you choose the type of thing you’re looking for
- You can now upload artist images. Photos only (no screenshots or downloaded images).
- New Venue layout, with iPad support!
- The journal can now be exported as JPGs to share to social media or whatever
- Tour posters can now be viewed and uploaded
- Search is now in the Discover page
- Genres have colors, now! (If you don’t like them, they can be disabled in the user menu.)
- iOS 18 support — sorry that took so long!

35 Github issues! Several bug fixes. I’m sure there’s stuff I forgot to mention.

A ton of work has also been done in Open Concert DB to fetch data more reliably. After you use the app for a bit, you may start noting your area being more filled out and up to date.
